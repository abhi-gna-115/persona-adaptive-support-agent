Title: Billing Support Guide

Users may experience billing issues such as failed payments, duplicate charges, or invoice discrepancies.

Recommended steps:
1. Verify payment method details.
2. Confirm sufficient account balance.
3. Review recent transactions.
4. Check whether payment was declined by the bank.

Escalate if:
- Duplicate charges occurred.
- Refund requests are involved.
- Legal or financial disputes are raised.
- Enterprise customer billing is affected.
