Title: Account Lock Troubleshooting

Accounts may be temporarily locked after multiple failed login attempts.
This is done to protect the user account from unauthorized access.

A temporary lock usually lasts for 30 minutes.
After 30 minutes, the user can try logging in again with the correct password.

Recommended steps:
1. Wait 30 minutes before trying again.
2. Reset the password if the user is unsure of the current password.
3. Avoid repeated login attempts during the lock period.
4. Check whether Caps Lock is enabled.
5. Confirm that the correct registered email address is being used.

Escalate the issue if:
- The account remains locked after 30 minutes.
- The user reports suspicious login activity.
- The user cannot access the registered email address.
- The issue affects a business-critical account.
